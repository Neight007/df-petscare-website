DF Bio-Fix

Restore Balance. Rebuild Health.

Powered by Saccharomyces boulardii (S. boulardii)

DF Bio-Fix is built around Saccharomyces boulardii, a clinically validated probiotic yeast sourced from France's Lesaffre — the global leader in yeast biotechnology. Unlike bacterial probiotics, S. boulardii is naturally resistant to antibiotics, making it the ideal companion during and after antibiotic therapy.

Whether your pet is fighting acute diarrhea or needs daily digestive support, DF Bio-Fix delivers precision where it matters most — the gut.

⚡

Acute Diarrhea & Antibiotic Support

In cases of acute bacterial diarrhea, S. boulardii works alongside antibiotics — not against them. It helps restore gut flora balance, reduces the duration of diarrhea, and prevents antibiotic-associated dysbiosis. Clinically proven to shorten recovery time significantly.

🌱

Daily Care & Chronic GI Support

For pets with chronic soft stools, sensitive stomachs, or weakened GI function, DF Bio-Fix serves as a daily microbiome maintenance formula. Regular supplementation strengthens intestinal barrier integrity and supports long-term digestive resilience.
