01 / Understanding the pet microbiome

A tiny world with an important role.

Every cat and dog is home to communities of bacteria, fungi and other microorganisms. Together with their genes and environment, they form the microbiome.

These communities interact with digestion, immune function and the body’s natural barriers. A healthy microbiome is about a functional ecosystem—not simply more “good” bacteria.

Explore the bigger picture

Microorganisms interact with each other and their hosts in complex ways. Supporting microbial function means considering the animal, its diet and the environment as a whole. Understanding these interactions is the foundation of our approach.

03 / Our formulation philosophy

Every ingredient needs a purpose.

◉

Evidence-Led Selection

We examine relevant scientific literature, ingredient characteristics, safety information, and available research in companion animals.

✧

Purposeful Ingredients

Each ingredient should have a clear function within the formulation. We favor meaningful ingredient combinations over unnecessary complexity.

⊕

Thoughtful Formulation

We consider ingredient compatibility, appropriate dosage, stability, and the practical needs of cats and dogs.

✓

Quality-Minded Development

We value reliable suppliers, ingredient traceability, suitable manufacturing processes, and meaningful quality verification.

Good formulation is not simply about what goes into a product. It is also about why each ingredient belongs there.

04 / Our approach to quality

Quality starts with the details.

Clear information. Thoughtful checks. Confidence in the details.

Know the ingredients

Ingredient identity, traceability and qualified suppliers.

Check the formulation

Relevant safety evidence, ingredient compatibility and stability.

Build for consistency

Suitable manufacturing, meaningful verification and responsible communication.

What we consider for live microorganisms

We pay particular attention to strain identification, viable cell counts and stability throughout the intended shelf life. As our collections develop, we aim to build quality practices that support transparency and confidence.

05 / Science & evidence

Curious by nature.
Guided by evidence.

Microbiome science is continually evolving, particularly in companion animal health.

We believe responsible innovation requires both an appreciation of scientific discoveries and an honest understanding of what remains unknown.

Through our educational resources, we aim to share relevant research, explain emerging microbiome concepts, and help pet parents make more informed decisions.

We distinguish between general microbiome research, ingredient-specific evidence, and studies conducted on finished products.
