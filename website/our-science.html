01 / Understanding the pet microbiome

A tiny world with an important role.

Every dog and cat is home to a diverse community of microorganisms, including bacteria, fungi, viruses, and other microbes.

Together with their genetic material and surrounding environment, these microbial communities form what we call the microbiome.

Far from being simply "good" or "bad," microorganisms interact with each other and their hosts in complex ways. They contribute to important biological processes, including digestion, immune system function, and the maintenance of healthy barriers.

A healthy microbiome is not about eliminating bacteria or maximizing a single type of beneficial microorganism. It is about supporting a functional microbial ecosystem that works in harmony with the animal's body.

At DF Pet Care, understanding these complex interactions is the foundation of our approach to pet wellness.

03 / Our formulation philosophy

Every ingredient needs a purpose.

◉

Evidence-Led Selection

We examine relevant scientific literature, ingredient characteristics, safety information, and available research in companion animals.

✧

Purposeful Ingredients

Each ingredient should have a clear function within the formulation. We favor meaningful ingredient combinations over unnecessary complexity.

⊕

Thoughtful Formulation

We consider ingredient compatibility, appropriate dosage, stability, and the practical needs of cats and dogs.

✓

Quality-Minded Development

We value reliable suppliers, ingredient traceability, suitable manufacturing processes, and meaningful quality verification.

Good formulation is not simply about what goes into a product. It is also about why each ingredient belongs there.

04 / Our approach to quality

Quality starts with the details.

We believe pet parents deserve clear information about the products they choose.

Our approach to product development places importance on:

Ingredient identity and traceability

Supplier qualification and quality documentation

Appropriate safety and scientific evidence

Formulation compatibility and stability

Manufacturing quality and product consistency

Clear, responsible product communication

For products containing live microorganisms, particular attention is given to strain identification, viable cell counts, and stability throughout the intended shelf life.

As we develop our collections, we aim to build quality practices that support transparency and confidence in every product.

05 / Science & evidence

Curious by nature.
Guided by evidence.

Microbiome science is continually evolving, particularly in companion animal health.

We believe responsible innovation requires both an appreciation of scientific discoveries and an honest understanding of what remains unknown.

Through our educational resources, we aim to share relevant research, explain emerging microbiome concepts, and help pet parents make more informed decisions.

We distinguish between general microbiome research, ingredient-specific evidence, and studies conducted on finished products.
